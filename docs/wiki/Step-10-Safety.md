# Step 10: Mission safety over time

![Step 10: Mission safety over time](https://git.tirsystem.com/Tirsvad-Udemy-100-days-of-code/099-assignment-analyse-and-visualise-the-space-race/raw/branch/main/docs/images/10-mission-safety-over-time.png)

## What the step does

The failure rate per year and per decade answers whether missions have become safer.

## What was gained

- Yes: 69% of launches failed in the 1950s, 21% in the 1960s and 6% to 10% since the 1970s.
- Grouping by decade smooths the year-to-year noise that a few launches cause.
- A rate needs its count beside it: the 1950s figure rests on only 51 launches.
