# Step 5: Launch prices and money spent

![Step 5: Launch prices and money spent](https://git.tirsystem.com/Tirsvad-Udemy-100-days-of-code/099-assignment-analyse-and-visualise-the-space-race/raw/branch/main/docs/images/05-launch-prices-and-money-spent.png)

## What the step does

A histogram shows the spread of launch prices, and two bar charts show the money spent and the average price per launch for each organisation.

## What was gained

- The median known price is 62 million USD, but one organisation (RVSN USSR) has an average of 5,000 million, an outlier that dominates the average chart.
- NASA spent the most in total (about 76,280 million USD) because it has many priced launches.
- Total and average answer different questions; showing both avoids a misleading picture.
